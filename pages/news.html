WorldSkills Excellence

After a year of rigorous preparation for the WorldSkills Kazan 2019 competition, our students achieved outstanding results. Congratulations to our students and their mentors on these globally recognized achievements!

Scholarship Winners Revealed

Digital Summer Camp

Algebra Junior is back with the Digital Summer Camp! Elementary school students can join us in Crikvenica or Zagreb for fun, educational workshops focused on digital design, multimedia, and technology. It's a perfect opportunity for children to learn mass media creation and journalist skills while enjoying their summer break at the sea or in the city!
