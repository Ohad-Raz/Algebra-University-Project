About Algebra University

Our History

Together we build the history of Algebra. We started almost twenty years ago and with you we continue the path towards new successes.

We opened Algebra LAB, a unique innovation center that helps companies, public services and start-up projects develop innovative business solutions that will boost the competitiveness of the economy. With an investment of three million kuna, we are the only ones in Croatia to unite three key elements – a department of applied research and development in the digital field, a start-up incubator with coworking space and an innovative educational platform intended for spreading the most advanced technological and business knowledge. Algebra LAB is a new place for innovation, creation, ideas and dreams about the future.

Algebra d.o.o. was founded in April 1998 as a company for adult education. In June 1998. it records the first participants of computer application and computer applications seminars. In September 1998. it records the first significant increase in the number of service users, so that through multi-digit growth in 1999 and 2000. it established itself in the adult education market, in the field of end-user application of information technologies (IT).

After much effort and hard work we convinced in 2000 the local office Microsoft Croatia that we meet the set conditions so we could become an authorized training center for their technologies. This, the first in a series, authorization distanced us from other education providers from that time and secured us access to official teaching materials and more favorable software licensing conditions. We subsequently significantly improved this status to reach the "Gold" epithet.

Encouraged by the success of obtaining Microsoft accreditation, in 2001 we put all our strength into meeting the conditions for establishing an official training center for other manufacturers and in the same year we became: Macromedia Authorized Training Center Adobe Authorized Training Provider Autodesk Authorized Training Center and IBM Business Partner.

At the beginning of 2002, Algebra d.o.o. founded the Algebra Learning Center, and in the same year moved to a significantly larger and higher quality space at Maksimirska 58a, Zagreb where a total of 6 classrooms and about 800 m2 of furnished educational space are made available to participants. According to estimates by Microsoft Croatia, Algebra's share in the Croatian market of educational services for Microsoft tools grew that year to 30%–35%.

After more than 2 years of intensive preparation, at the end of 2007, the College of Applied Computing was founded, which received a permit from the Ministry of Science, Education and Sports in June 2008. This event set new development guidelines for the Algebra education group and in a certain way successfully rounded off efforts to launch a higher education program that were initiated back in 2002 through the implementation of the IBM ACE education program. The implementation of the teaching program begins in the premises of FER and in the Algebra building on Maksimirska Street.

We further expanded our capacities by adding new classrooms at the address in Ilica, and we successfully passed the quality check of program implementation in the organization of FER and extended the certificate "Approved by FER" until 2016. The entire Algebra group was rated as the leading ECDL center in Croatia, and we were also highlighted as an example in the implementation of EU projects which was crowned by the visit of EU Commissioner Mr. Andor. Algebra University has had a new logo and visual identity since this year.

After two years of intensive preparation and the conducted initial accreditation procedure, Algebra University received in January 2015. permits for a new undergraduate and graduate Study of Digital Marketing, the first in this part of Europe. We know there is still much to do ahead of us. We know that we can only sometimes turn around and look back to remember the beautiful moments that marked our almost 20 years of work. Thank you for believing in us all these years and helping us grow.

We integrated into our system one of the first Croatian business schools: International Graduate Business School (IgBS Zagreb) and we are introducing into our program a graduate MBA study of eLeadership (eLeadership) in English. This study is largely taught by lecturers from the University of Indiana, Kelley School of Business, one of the five leading American business schools according to the prestigious business magazine Bloomberg, while some specific topics are reserved for Croatian and European professors.

About Algebra Group

Algebra group is the leading Croatian and regional private education system present in 30 cities throughout the Republic of Croatia, with more than 120 permanent employees and more than 400 external collaborators.

Over the past almost 20 years of work, Algebra has become the most significant regional education partner of companies Microsoft, Cisco, Adobe, Autodesk, ECDL, VMware, EC-Council and others. In adult education we train about 18,000 participants in seminars and education programs annually, while in higher education we enroll each year more than 300 new students. The quality of Algebra's business has been recognized by both our clients and all major software manufacturers, and it is proven by the implemented ISO 9001:2000 certificate which we introduced back in 2004. year.
